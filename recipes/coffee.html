Quick Coffee

Description

This coffee recipe is super quick to follow and tastes good as well. Almost everyone I know can make coffee this way.

Ingredients

  • 2rs nescafe coffee, or any coffee you want
  • One cup milk
  • Sugar

Steps

  1. Pour a cup of milk in a pot and start heating it.
  2. Pour 2 sachets of nescafe coffee in the pot.
  3. Add sugar according to your preference. I prefer one teaspoon of sugar.
  4. Boil it while stirring, and stop boiling when it's hot enough to drink
  5. Enjoy!
